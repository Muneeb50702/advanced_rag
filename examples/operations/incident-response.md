# Incident response

When an alert fires, the on-call engineer acknowledges it within fifteen minutes and records the incident start time. They assess customer impact before changing production systems.

For a severity-one incident, open an incident channel, name an incident commander, and post a status update every thirty minutes. The commander owns the timeline and communication; the responder owns diagnosis and mitigation.

After service is restored, write a blameless postmortem within three working days. Include the trigger, customer impact, detection gap, actions taken, and follow-up owners.
